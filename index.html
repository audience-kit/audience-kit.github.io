The platform for community apps

Give every scene its own home.

AudienceKit gives a community its own app: its own name, domain and look, with the venues, events and people that make it real. You bring the people. We handle the rest.

Launch an audience Read the docs

What you get

Everything a scene needs, on one platform

Each audience is its own place with its own people. The plumbing underneath is shared, so a new one is ready on day one.

Your name, your domain, your look

Every audience gets its own domain, accent colour and spectrum. Members see your community, not ours.

The places that matter

Map the bars, clubs and spaces where your people go, grouped by city and neighbourhood.

Events and tickets

Publish nights, bring in listings from Facebook, Eventbrite and Ticketmaster, and let members RSVP.

The people who make the night

Give DJs, performers and hosts a page, and let members follow them, find friends and go out together.

Photos from the night

Photographers share their sets straight to the audiences they shoot for, so members can find themselves the next morning.

An admin console of your own

Organizers run their audience from its own console, skinned in its colours, with no admin experience needed.

How it works

From idea to app in three steps

Name your audience

Pick a name, a domain and a tagline in your own voice.

Make it yours

Choose a preset, then set your accent and spectrum. We check every colour for contrast so it stays readable.

Fill it with life

Add venues, events and people. We help keep listings fresh by finding new nights as they're announced.
